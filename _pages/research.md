## Research

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/im-selection.svg" class="research-thumb" alt="Intensity Measure Selection">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Intensity Measure Selection for PBEE</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Developing variance-based robustness criteria using Sobol decomposition of total risk variance and information-theoretic structural sufficiency criteria to replace ad-hoc intensity measure selection heuristics in performance-based earthquake engineering. Current extensions include distributionally robust selection via Wasserstein ambiguity sets and proper scoring rule decompositions.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/generative-design.svg" class="research-thumb" alt="Generative Structural Design">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Generative Models for Structural Design</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Implementing and benchmarking Conditional Flow Matching (CFM), DDPM, CVAE, cGAN/WGAN-GP, and CNF/RealNVP for inverse design of post-tensioned CLT rocking wall systems. GP-augmented training corpora have demonstrated simultaneous improvements in feasibility and recall. Ongoing work targets publication in CACIE and Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/active-learning.svg" class="research-thumb" alt="Active Learning">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Active Learning for Seismic Fragility Estimation</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Development of the Kernel-Integrated Fisher (KIF) acquisition function, which recovers GP-Var, D-Optimality, IFV, and MEF as limiting cases. Applications include OpenSeesPy SDOF demand surrogates using deep ensembles and cloud-analysis fragility estimation of post-tensioned CLT systems with efficient uncertainty quantification.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/morbdo.svg" class="research-thumb" alt="Reliability-Based Design Optimization">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Multi-Objective Reliability-Based Design Optimization</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Development of the first system-level FORM-based reliability-based design optimization framework for post-tensioned CLT structures integrating sustainability objectives through NSGA-II and Gaussian process surrogates. A multi-city study across the U.S. West Coast revealed substantial regional variability in embodied carbon impacts while maintaining target reliability levels.</p>
</div>
